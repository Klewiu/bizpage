# WickyWave Software

> A software house creating custom web applications, administration panels, and internal systems
> for B2B and B2C companies, based on Django, Angular, and modern APIs. We help businesses automate
> processes, integrate data across systems, and build software tailored to real business needs
> instead of the limitations of off-the-shelf tools.

WickyWave Software designs and delivers custom web applications, including administration panels,
customer portals, SaaS platforms, AI/ML models, and ERP/CRM integrations. The company also develops
an active SaaS product called Xoaila, which monitors brand visibility in traditional search engines
and AI-generated answers.
